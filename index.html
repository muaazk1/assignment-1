About this Guide

Hello! I'm a community correspondent who looks for the best events and activities happening in Greensboro.

Here are some of the events my guide covers:

  • Outdoor workshops and classes
  • Seasonal downtown gatherings & farmers markets
  • Local art exhibitions and live community performances
  • And more!

Visit the site regularly to stay up to date on the latest events happening in Greensboro!
